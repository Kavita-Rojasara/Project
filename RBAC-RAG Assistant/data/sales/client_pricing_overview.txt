Client Pricing Overview
-----------------------

This document provides a high-level overview of project pricing.

1. Pricing Model
Project pricing is generally based on:
- Project scope
- Timeline
- Team size
- Technical complexity

2. Pricing Tiers
- Small projects: ₹3–6 lakhs
- Medium projects: ₹8–15 lakhs
- Large projects: Custom pricing

3. Payment Structure
Payments are usually divided into milestones:
- Advance payment at project start
- Milestone-based payments
- Final payment on delivery

4. Confidentiality
Pricing information is confidential and should not be shared externally
without proper authorization.
