Salary Structure and Compensation Details
-----------------------------------------

This document contains confidential information related to employee compensation.
Access to this document is restricted to authorized personnel only.

1. Salary Components
Employee salary consists of the following components:
- Basic Pay
- House Rent Allowance (HRA)
- Special Allowance
- Performance Bonus (if applicable)

2. Salary Bands (Monthly - INR)

Junior Level (0–2 years experience):
- ₹30,000 – ₹50,000

Mid Level (2–5 years experience):
- ₹60,000 – ₹90,000

Senior Level (5+ years experience):
- ₹1,00,000 – ₹1,50,000

3. Performance Bonus
Performance bonuses are discretionary and based on:
- Individual performance
- Project delivery
- Company revenue

Bonuses are typically reviewed annually.

4. Salary Revisions
Salary revisions are conducted once a year.
Revisions depend on performance reviews and company growth.

5. Confidentiality Notice
Employees are strictly prohibited from sharing salary details with other employees.
Any violation of this policy may lead to disciplinary action.

This document is confidential and intended for internal use only.
