Company Leave Policy
--------------------

This document outlines the leave policy for all full-time employees of the company.
The policy is applicable to employees working from India.

1. Annual Leave (Paid Leave)
Employees are entitled to 18 paid leaves per calendar year.
Paid leaves can be used for personal reasons, travel, or rest.
Unused paid leaves can be carried forward up to a maximum of 10 days.

2. Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 sick leaves per year.
Sick leave can be used in case of illness or medical emergencies.
For sick leave longer than 3 consecutive days, medical proof may be requested.

3. Casual Leave
Employees are entitled to 6 casual leaves per year.
Casual leave is intended for short-term or urgent personal matters.
Casual leave cannot be carried forward to the next year.

4. Public Holidays
The company observes public holidays as per the Indian holiday calendar.
A list of official holidays is shared at the beginning of every year.

5. Maternity Leave
Female employees are eligible for maternity leave as per Government of India regulations.
Currently, maternity leave duration is up to 26 weeks.

6. Leave Approval Process
All leaves must be applied through the internal HR system.
Leave approval is subject to manager approval.
In case of emergency, employees should inform their manager as early as possible.

This policy may be updated based on company requirements and legal regulations.
